E-Vakeel

A mobile legal-aid platform connecting undertrial prisoners in India with pro-bono lawyers, bail and parole processes, and mental health support. Built for the Ministry of Law and Justice problem statement at Smart India Hackathon 2023.

RoleTeam lead
TimelineSep — Oct 2023
Team6 members (NoobCoderException), with an AI/ML mentor and a criminal law advocate
OutcomeHackathon prototype, not taken further

The problem

Undertrial prisoners are people held in jail while they await trial, often for longer than any eventual sentence would require. Many have no reliable access to a lawyer, their case status, or information about bail and parole. Problem statement 1282 from the Ministry of Law and Justice asked for tech-driven solutions to this gap.

75%of India’s prison population is undertrial
6user groups: prisoners, support persons, lawyers, judges, review committees and jail authorities

Research

We built from the legal frameworks the system already runs on: the Code of Criminal Procedure 1973, the Model Prison Manual 2016, undertrial review committee SOPs, Bar Council of India records and the e-Courts portal. We then mapped what each user needs before, during and after trial.

Decisions

Two apps, not one

E-Vakeel serves prisoners, support persons and lawyers. E-Vakeel Admin serves judges, review committees and jail authorities. A support person and a judge need different permissions and workflows, so we split them.

AI lawyer matching, with a manual fallback

The app suggests a lawyer automatically, but users can also filter and choose one themselves. With this much at stake, we didn’t want the assignment to be fully automatic.

Name the risks up front

We flagged three show-stoppers in the pitch: the system only works if every party uses it, it depends on government data, and language and literacy are real barriers. Regional language support was our answer to the last one.

What we built

A prototype of two linked mobile apps on ReactJS, Ionic and Capacitor. E-Vakeel covers lawyer assignment, a case dashboard, an LLM-based legal aid chatbot with voice input, digital bail and parole applications, and mental health referrals. E-Vakeel Admin covers a priority case dashboard, video trial setup and bail decisions.

What I'd redo

We named universal adoption as a show-stopper, then designed the full feature set anyway. A tool this dependent on every party using it needs a plan for getting there first. Next time I’d start with the smallest slice that helps one user group on its own, and build adoption from that.
